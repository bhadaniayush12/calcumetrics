# Research Log: Advance Tax Calculator (India Tax)

## 1. Statutory Grounding & Formula
Advance tax (commonly termed "pay-as-you-earn" tax) is governed by Sections 207 to 211 of the Income-tax Act, 1961. Every taxpayer whose estimated total tax liability for the financial year (after adjusting for TDS/TCS and rebates) equals or exceeds ₹10,000 is statutorily obligated to pay tax in quarterly installments.

- **Threshold Guard:**
  - $\text{Net Estimated Tax Liability} \ge ₹10,000 \implies$ Advance Tax Payable.
  - Senior citizens (age 60 or above) resident in India who do not have any income from business or profession are exempt from advance tax under Section 207(2).

- **Statutory Due Dates & Cumulative Installment Percentages (Section 211):**
  1. On or before **15th June**: Not less than **15%** of advance tax liability.
  2. On or before **15th September**: Not less than **45%** of advance tax liability (less tax paid in prior installment).
  3. On or before **15th December**: Not less than **75%** of advance tax liability (less tax paid in prior installments).
  4. On or before **15th March**: **100%** of advance tax liability (less tax paid in prior installments).

- **Interest for Default / Deferment:**
  - Section 234C: Simple interest @ 1% per month for deferment of installment amounts below required thresholds.
  - Section 234B: Simple interest @ 1% per month if less than 90% of assessed tax is paid by 31st March.
