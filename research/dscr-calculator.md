# Research Log: Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) Calculator

## 1. Mathematical Grounding & Formula
The Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) is a critical commercial banking and corporate solvency metric that measures a firm or income-generating property's capacity to cover its total debt obligations with its operational earnings.

- **Formula:**
  $$\text{DSCR} = \frac{\text{Net Operating Income (NOI)}}{\text{Total Debt Service}}$$
  Where:
  - $\text{Net Operating Income (NOI)}$ = Revenue − Operating Expenses (or EBIT / EBITDA).
  - $\text{Total Debt Service}$ = Principal Repayment + Interest Obligations due in the measurement period.

- **Underwriting Benchmarks:**
  - $\text{DSCR} \ge 2.0$: Strong liquidity cushion; easily serviceable.
  - $1.25 \le \text{DSCR} < 2.0$: Standard investment-grade threshold; required by most commercial banks and SBA lenders (minimum 1.20x to 1.25x).
  - $1.0 \le \text{DSCR} < 1.25$: Marginal / break-even; vulnerable to minor revenue contractions or rate hikes.
  - $\text{DSCR} < 1.0$: Negative cash flow relative to debt; borrower cannot service debt from operations alone.
